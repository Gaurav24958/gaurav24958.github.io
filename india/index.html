Chapter One

Gaurav Boob

A |

Software engineer from Nashik, India — now in Dublin.

I build systems that move money, and I research models that help doctors see better. Two years at FIS on payment infrastructure - ACH, Wire, and FedWire systems serving US banking clients including State Street Bank. Before that, deep learning research at PICT: lipreading models for Indian accent English and diabetic retinopathy classification from retinal images, both making it to Springer. Two hackathon wins in AI, competing against 200+ teams each time.

I got into tech the way I got into everything else - by pulling things apart to see what was inside. That has not changed. I still build outside of work for the same reason I built as a kid: something is broken, or something does not exist yet, and that is enough reason to start. Currently at Trinity College Dublin for an MSc in Computer Science, going deep into machine learning and distributed systems — the theoretical foundation I always knew I was building toward.

What I work on outside of screens: trails I have not been on before, a camera pointed at things most people walk past, self-help books I finish faster than I expect to, and the occasional deep dive into Naval or Robert Greene at midnight.

Still figuring out which hard problem deserves the next decade.

Currently MSc CS at Trinity College Dublin
