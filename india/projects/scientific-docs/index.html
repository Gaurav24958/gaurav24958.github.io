Executive Summary & Problem Statement

Academic research papers present severe challenges for standard Document Q&A and RAG applications. Conventional PDF parsers and OCR tools (like Tesseract) corrupt multi-column layout flows, transform dense mathematical formulas into meaningless ASCII strings, and scramble embedded scientific tables into unreadable blocks.

PaperInsight solved this critical information extraction bottleneck. We fine-tuned Meta's Nougat (Neural Optical Understanding for Academic Documents) visual transformer model to convert page screenshots directly into semantically clean Markdown and LaTeX syntax, preserving equations, matrices, and tables with zero loss. Coupled with high-density vector retrieval and Gemini 1.0 Pro, researchers can converse intuitively with complex technical literature.

Technical Architecture & Pipeline

Step 01 Visual Renderer

High-Res Page Rasterization

Renders multi-page PDF documents into high-DPI image representations to feed visual transformer attention mechanisms.

Why it matters: Prevents PDF font encoding corruption and preserves inline figure references.
Step 02 Nougat Visual Transformer

Optical LaTeX & Markdown Parsing

Fine-tuned vision transformer transcribes complex multi-column scientific layouts, mathematical symbols, and tables directly into structured Markdown and LaTeX.

Why it matters: Guarantees zero loss of semantic meaning in mathematical formulas, chemical equations, and tabular data.
Step 03 Equation-Aware Splitter

Semantic Chunking & Embedding

Splits parsed Markdown along natural section, proof, and theorem boundaries rather than arbitrary token character counts.

Why it matters: Keeps mathematical formulas and explanatory context unified within single vector embeddings.
Step 04 Vector DB + BM25

Hybrid Dense-Sparse Retrieval

Combines cosine semantic search with BM25 keyword matching to retrieve relevant paper excerpts, proofs, and references.

Why it matters: Ensures high recall for both broad thematic concepts and precise mathematical symbol searches.
Step 05 Gemini 1.0 Pro

Grounded Synthesis & Citation

Synthesizes clear, factual answers grounded strictly in retrieved context, providing exact page-level and theorem-level references.

Why it matters: Completely eliminates hallucinations and enables rapid verification for academic researchers.
